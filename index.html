The Studio

Every space should feel deeply personal.

Velour Interiors is an interior design studio serving clients across Canada and the United States. We specialize in creating luxurious, sophisticated, and effortlessly livable spaces that balance timeless aesthetics with everyday functionality.

We believe every space should feel deeply personal. Rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach, each project is thoughtfully developed from the ground up to reflect our clients’ individual tastes, lifestyles, and aspirations.

Our holistic approach brings every essential element of the design process together. From establishing a cohesive creative direction and optimizing layouts and spatial flow to developing architectural details and selecting finishes, fixtures, lighting, furniture, accessories, and artwork, every decision is made with intention.

To ensure the design vision is carried through seamlessly, we remain closely involved at every stage; from initial concepts and design development to construction coordination, installation, furniture placement, and final styling.

Our goal is to create distinctive interiors that feel elevated, functional, beautifully considered, and uniquely yours.

Founder · Gia Oberoi

Principal Designer

Rational architecture. Emotional design.

Born and raised in Chandigarh — Le Corbusier’s modernist masterpiece — Gia draws on cultural richness and architectural history. Her signature blends modern and eclectic elements with luxurious finishes and custom furnishings.

Known for bold color, layered pattern, and global influence, she designs spaces that nurture lifestyle and well-being — each vignette a functional work of art.
